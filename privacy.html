Privacy

Effective 17 Sep 2026. Operator: Rahul Deep (SecureOps desk). This page is the ad-destination privacy notice. It is not a law-firm opinion.

What we collect

From the engagement challan or a Meta/X lead form: name, company, email, WhatsApp number, the URL/IP/app you say you own, service requested, and free-text notes. We do not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, or broker passwords on this form.

Why

To decide if we can take the job, send AUTH / RoE / quote, invoice, and deliver a report. We do not sell leads. We do not use the form to scan your site automatically.

Where it lives

Until a public host is wired, submissions on the local preview are stored as JSON on the operator’s desk (BBH/business/desk/leads/) on an encrypted working disk. If this site is on GitHub Pages, the browser downloads JSON and/or opens mail to the published address — GitHub does not receive the lead body. If a Meta lead form is used, Meta also holds the lead under their terms; we copy it into the same desk.

Sharing

We share engagement data only with: you; payment providers you pay (UPI/bank); and, if a dispute or legal duty requires it, Indian authorities. Subcontractors, if ever used, would be under NDA and only on the signed scope.

Retention

Quotes that never sign: delete on request or within 24 months. Signed jobs, invoices, and reports: kept as needed for tax and IT Act records (typically up to 8 years).

Your control

To access, correct, or delete a lead that has not become a signed job, DM the same SecureOps social account you used. We do not publish a phone number or inbox on this site. Signed-job files follow the contract.

Cookies and ads

This static site does not set a tracking pixel until the operator adds one after a public URL exists. If a Meta pixel or conversion API is added later, this page will be updated the same day. Ads will not claim investment returns.

Children

This is a B2B service. We do not target people under 18.

Paid lead ads still need this page on a public https URL. Organic posts can use DM without publishing a number.
